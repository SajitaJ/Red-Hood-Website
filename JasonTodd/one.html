Robin Origins

Post Crisis (currently canon)

After his father, Willis, was arrested and his mother, Catherine, died of an overdose, he lived on the streets, avoiding Gotham's foster care system knowing it was corrupt. When he was around 11 years old, he attempted to steal the tires off the Batmobile, only being caught by Batman when he went back to steal the fourth tire. Bruce originally sends Jason to a school for boys run by Ma Gunn, but after finding out it's front for turning boys into criminals, Batman and Jason work together to put a stop to the school. Bruce then adopts Jason and trains him for around 6 months and when he’s around 12 years old he officially becomes the second Robin.

Pre Crisis

While this was retconned, Jason originally had an origin story that was similar to his predecessor, Dick Grayson. Jason along with his parents were in the circus, and when his parents died in a circus accident he was taken in by Bruce. He originally had red hair that he dyed black to more resemble Dick Grayson. This version of him was mainly created so that Dick could still be Nightwing and have his own storylines, while allowing Batman to still have a Robin. In this era Jason was just a copy of his older brother.

The Death of Jason Todd

Jason was super excited to be the next Robin, even saying “I'm Robin and Robin gives me magic”. He was smart and quick witted, albeit a bit reckless, but overall a hopeful kid. However, after the storyline in Batman #424, where a criminal can’t be prosecuted due to diplomatic immunity and his victim, Gloria, commits suicide out of fear that her rapist will come after her, Jason begins to see the worst in the world. When Jason goes to confront the criminal, Felipe Garzonas, Felipe ends up falling out the window and dying. Batman believes Jason pushed him, though Jason is adamant that Felipe fell. After this incident, Jason became more reckless and violent when out as Robin, which eventually led to Batman benching him as Robin.

Jason runs away and returns to Crime Alley where he grew up. While there, he meets one of his old neighbours who gives him a box of his parents' old belongings that she was able to save from their old apartment. He ends up finding his birth certificate and discovers that Catherine Todd was not his birth mother, due to damage to the papers, all he knows is that his mothers name starts with an S. He finds his fathers phone book in the belongings and searches for who his potential mother could be and finds three options: Sharmin Rosen, Sandra Wu-San, and Sheila Haywood. Jason ends up leaving a note for Alfred Pennyworth and books flights to meet each of the three women. He ends up running into Batman who was searching for the Joker. Together, they narrow down the list until Sheila Haywood is the only one left.

In Ethiopia, they meet Sheila and confirm that she is, indeed, Jason's birth mother. Batman ends up leaving him alone to spend time with his mother while he goes to find and stop the Joker. However Jason finds out his mother is being blackmailed by the Joker. He reveals to her that he is Robin to try to help her, but instead she betrays him, handing him over to the Joker. He beats Jason with a crowbar as his mother watches, and eventually sets a bomb. The Joker betrays Sheila, tying her up and leaving them both to die in the warehouse. With what little strength Jason has left, he attempts to save his mother, untying her and making it to the door, only to realize it was locked.

At the same time Batman was on his way over to try to save Jason, but in the end he only makes it in time to watch as the warehouse explodes. He searches the rubble only to find Jason's dead body.

Jason Todd was 15, standing at 4’6”, weighing only 87 pounds when he died on April 27.

Red Hood Origins

6 months after his death, Jason wakes up in his grave, still injured and oxygen deprived. He claws his way out, using his belt buckle to break through the wood of the coffin and digs his way through the mud until he reaches the surface. He wanders around, delirious, until he gets hit by a car and taken to a hospital. The staff are unable to identify him and Jason himself is in a coma, so he’s stuck at the hospital as a John Doe until he eventually wakes up and escapes. However, his mind was not fully there, he didn't know who he was and didn’t react to anything but danger. His body was acting on instinct, using muscle memory from his days on the streets and as Robin to survive and fight off any threats.

Eventually Talia al Ghul finds him and takes him to the League of Assassins, in hopes of helping him heal and giving him to Bruce as a ‘gift’. However Jason does not heal, he stays in his catatonic state, only fighting off threats. In the end, Talia dumps him in the Lazarus Pits to heal him. But Jason comes back angry, especially after finding out that not only did Batman not kill the Joker, but he also replaced Jason with a shiny new Robin, Tim Drake.

Jason ends up training around the world with different experts in marksmanship, demolitions, martial arts and poisons before returning to Gotham as the Red Hood.

‘Red Hood’ was Joker's old moniker, and Jason uses the name to not only get Batman's attention but also the Joker himself. Almost overnight, the Red Hood takes over Gotham's underworld through intimidation and offering protection from Batman and other costumed villains. He slowly leaves hints for Batman as to his identity while also antagonizing Black Mask into breaking Joker out of Arkham to deal with him. His plan nearly succeeds when he kidnaps Joker and has a confrontation with Batman. He wants Batman to prove he cares for Jason by killing Joker, but the confrontation ends with Batman throwing a batarang and slitting Jason's throat, and the building they were in explodes.

All three survive the incident, though it is theorized that Jason died and came back to life once again
