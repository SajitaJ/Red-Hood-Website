Glossary

Alfred Pennyworth

The Wayne family butler, though he practically raised Bruce after the death of his parents.


All-Caste

An ancient group of warrior monks, known for protecting the world against dark forces.


Arkham Asylum

A phychiatric hospital/prison for the mentally unstable villains of Gotham.


Batarang

A bat shaped throwing weapon used by Batman.


Batman/Bruce Wayne

After the death of his parents, Bruce Wayne trained around the world to become Gothams protector, Batman.


Batmobile

Batmans car.


Black Mask

A crime lord in Gotham City who leads a gang called the False Facers


Crime Alley

The street where Thomas and Martha Wayne were murdered. While it was originally known as Park Row, after the murder the street became known for crime and poverty, now called Crime Alley by Gotham residents.


Faye "Ma" Gunn

A Gotham city criminal mastermind who runs a crime academy masquerading as a boarding school.


Joker

Batmans arch-nemesis, he represents everything Batman fights against.


Lady Shiva/Sandra Wu-San

One of the worlds greatest assassins.


Lazarus Pits

Mystical pools around the world that can heal injuries, cure diseases and even grant immortality to those who bathe in it. They are most commonly associated with Ra's al Ghul and the League of Assassins.


League of Assassins

An ancient organization of assassins and killers run by Ra's al Ghul


Nightwing/Dick Grayson

Bruce Wayne's first ward and the original Robin. After his parents were murdered he was taken in by Bruce and became Robin to find his parents murderer. After being fired as Robin, he became Nightwing.


The Outlaws

A team of anti-heros led by the Red Hood. The original members were Red Hood, Arsenal, and Starfire. Red Hood then joined forces with Artemis and Bizarro in the most recent Outlaws.


Red Hood

An anti-hero, Jason Todd, who took up one of Jokers old monikers. Originally it referred to the Red Hood Gang, a criminal organization from Batman's early days. Their most notable member was the man who would become the Joker.


Red Robin/Tim Drake

The third Robin and Bruce Waynes adoptive son who deduced the identities of Batman and his Robins as a child. When Batman began acting recklessly after the death of Jason Todd, Tim tried to convince Dick Grayson to become Robin again. When that failed he decided to take up the mantle himself. After Nightwing gives Robin to Damian, Tim becomes Red Robin.


Robin

Batman's sidekick, there have been many over the years. The canon mainline Robins being: Dick Grayson, Jason Todd, Tim Drake, Stephanie Brown, and Damian Wayne


Sheila Haywood

She was a doctor in Gotham city who was in a relationship with Willis Todd. After the birth of Jason Todd, she left Gotham. While in Ethiopia she was being blackmailed by Joker who had evidence that she was embezzling money.


Superboy Prime

A superhero turned supervillain who originated from an alternate Earth. During his time as a villain he punched through reality, which created parallel timelines and altered the history of various events and characters.


Talia al Ghul

Daughter of Ra's al Ghul and a member of the League of Assassins. She is also Bruce Wayne's ex whom he has a child with.
