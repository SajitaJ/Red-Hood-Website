Jason Todd - The Red Hood

In the comic book community there is a well known saying: "Nobody stays dead in comics except Bucky Barnes, Uncle Ben, and Jason Todd.” Of course in recent years, this no longer applies as both Bucky and Jason Todd returned from the dead.

However, amongst these tragic characters, Jason Todd stands out, as his fate wasn't decided by the writers, but by the fans. In 1988, a telephone poll was held to determine the fate of Jason Todd, the second Robin. In the end, fans voted to kill him off only 5 years after his initial introduction, winning by a mere 72 votes.

There were two main reasons for this outcome. The first being, fans didn’t like Jason as much as the original Robin, Dick Grayson. The second being that most fans didn't believe that DC would ever actually go through with killing off such an important character. However, DC proved them wrong with the release of ‘A Death in the Family’, a storyline that led to the death of Jason Todd by the hands of the Joker.

This story, along with ‘Under the Red Hood’, which was released in 2005, became two of Jason’s most popular and well written comics.

Who Is Jason Todd

Jason Peter Todd was born on August 16 to Willis Todd and Sheila Haywood, though he was raised by Willis and his wife Catherine Todd, unaware that Catherine was his step-mom. Jason grew up in Crime Alley, in Gotham, though eventually gets adopted by Bruce Wayne and becomes Robin.

His first appearance was made in 1983, though his origins since his debut have changed. (Check out Origins for more information.)

After his death and resurrection, he became known as the Red Hood, originally taking control of a large portion of Gotham's criminal underworld, though he is now closer to a vigilante/antihero.

His affiliations include:

  • Batman
  • League of Assassins (former)
  • All-Caste (former)
  • The Outlaws

Fun Facts about Jason Todd

  • Jason resurrection was actually due to Superboy Prime punching through reality which changed many events including Jason’s death
  • Jason was the first human to train with the All-Caste, a secret order of magic monks, and is able to wield the ‘All-Blades’, fire blades made from his soul
  • Jason was only around 19 years old when he took over Gotham's underworld and became the Red Hood, though he could technically be considered younger since his spent 6 months dead and over a year in a coma and catatonic.
