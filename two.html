Comics

This section mainly covers comics centred around Jason or where he plays a large role. While there are a lot of other comics where he makes appearances, these are some the ones to read to understand Jason's character.

Robin Comics

  • Batman: Second Chances - An introduction to Jason Todd
  • Detective Comics Batman #534
  • Batman: The Cult
  • Robin & Batman: Jason Todd
  • Batman: The New Adventures #414, #421, #422
  • Batman: A Death In The Family - An end to Jasons time as Robin

Red Hood Comics

Here are some of the pre New 52 comics:

  • Batman: Under the Red Hood - An introduction to the Red Hood
  • Batman Annual 25
  • Batman: Red Hood - The Lost Days - While this takes place between Jasons resurrection and becomes Red Hood, it's best read after 'Under the Red Hood'
  • Teen Titans #29
  • Nightwing: Brothers in Blood
  • Green Arrow #69-72
  • Outsiders #44-46

These are the New 52 comics, though they do change Jason's characterization a lot:

  • Red Hood and the Outlaws
  • Batman and Robin #10-12, #17, #20, #33-37
  • Red Hood/Arsenal
  • Red Hood: Outlaw
  • Red Hood #51-52
  • Task Force Z
  • Red Hood: The Hill
